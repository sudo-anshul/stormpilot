# StormPilot design direction

Audience: an analyst or researcher examining a stormwater control policy, with a hackathon judge as a first-time user. Primary task: run a precise experiment, locate a failure, inspect its explanation and export evidence.

Direction: a technical field notebook combining the clarity of an engineering plot with the quiet structure of an atlas. The working result is the focal point. No invented city map, fake telemetry, customer logos or decorative impact statistics.

Composition: compact masthead; left experiment rail; large central statement and aligned hydrographs; contextual topological network inset; evidence detail on selection; comparison and replay controls below. The setup can collapse into the top of narrow layouts.

Typography: clear sans-serif for instructions and conclusions; compact monospace for units, time, asset IDs and provenance. Use a small number of purposeful type roles. Measurements receive tabular numerals.

Color: warm near-white paper, dark ink, blue/teal for water and nominal policy, amber/orange for perturbation, violet or dark blue for fallback. Failure requires text/icon as well as color. Borders and gridlines are quiet, charts remain legible without low-contrast decoration.

Geometry: square or lightly rounded panels, ruled separators, precise plotting areas and restrained spacing. Avoid a repeating collection of large rounded metric cards.

Imagery: original SVG network and time-series graphics computed from actual data. The topology is identified as a benchmark schematic, not a geographical flood map.

Motion: functional transitions only—progress, selected time/asset and panel changes. Honor reduced motion. No autoplay cinematic map or water animation unsupported by model outputs.

State contract: absent values stay unavailable; running work has honest progress; failures are retryable; completed results expose their source. Review desktop, narrow screen, keyboard behavior and error states after the first complete flow.

Applied skill: installed design-direction. Detailed frontend guidance and interface-design-review will be used during implementation and rendered verification.
